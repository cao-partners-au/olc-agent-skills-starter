# What's new in this edition (20 Oct 2026)

Compared with the earlier bundle you received:

**New skills**
- **meta-account-healthcheck** - start here if you already run Meta ads: a read-only, scored audit of your account history (ownership, tracking, structure, performance, fatigue, placements, Andromeda creative alignment out of 16, compliance) with the top 5 fixes and a 30-day plan.
- **meta-weekly-review** - a Monday review of your Meta ads that ends in keep / scale / pause / fix / hold for every ad, judged on what each ad's leads did in your CRM (not just cost per lead), with hold rules for Meta's learning phase, a fatigue test, and an ad-count ceiling. Includes a read-only data script.
- **marketing-roas** - joins ad spend, lead source and paid revenue into an honest return on ad spend.
- **cac-and-conversion** - cost per paying customer (paid and blended), payback for any revenue model (one-off sale, subscription, deposit/milestone, commission), breakeven cost per lead, and where deals stall.
- **meta-ad-recon** - free competitor ad research from Meta's Ad Library: what competitors run longest, and why.

**Updated skills**
- **meta-ads-campaign** - new creative playbook for how Meta now picks audiences from the ad itself (broad targeting, identity keywords, 3 to 5 new statics a week, concepts not copies, "zombie" ad set, phone testing). **Correction:** reading your own Page/Instagram posts and each ad's destination URL does NOT need Meta App Review. Added Meta's AI text-lock workaround and a destination-URL check after every edit.
- **meta-pixel-funnel / ad-pixel-funnel** - new rule: never fire real conversion events to test a live page (they count as real and can't be deleted); how to spot a stale test code.
- **marketing-roas** - compare equal, mature time windows; the "quality trap" (platforms reward cheap form fills; the CRM wins).
- **google-ads-campaign-launch / google-ads-conversions** - examples made industry-neutral.

**Unchanged:** open-operator-audit, tiktok-ads-mcp.

Method credit for the weekly review, creative playbook, window and payback rules: adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT).
