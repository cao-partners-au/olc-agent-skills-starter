# Termination Letter — Pre-Draft Checklist

Before drafting any termination letter, collect ALL of the following. Do not start drafting until every item is confirmed.

## Mandatory Inputs

| Item | Why needed |
|---|---|
| Employee full name | Addressee, salutation |
| Role / title | Letter body |
| Employing entity | Letterhead, signatory |
| Reason for termination | Performance / misconduct / redundancy / end of probation / mutual |
| Length of service (start date) | Calculates NES minimum notice period |
| Notice arrangement | Working notice vs payment in lieu |
| Notice period (weeks) | NES s117 — see table in skill |
| Last day of employment | Explicit statement required |
| Outstanding annual leave balance | Must be paid out on termination |
| Super status | Confirm up to date to termination date |
| Serious misconduct? | Yes = summary dismissal (no notice), must document specific conduct |
| Prior warnings issued? | Required for performance dismissal procedural fairness |
| Signing director | Which director signs |
| Date of letter | |

## NES Minimum Notice Quick Reference

| Service | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| Under 1 year | 1 week |
| 1-3 years | 2 weeks |
| 3-5 years | 3 weeks |
| Over 5 years | 4 weeks |
| Over 45 years old + 2+ years service | +1 week additional |

Note: for a small business (fewer than 15 employees), the unfair dismissal minimum employment period is 12 months.

## Reason-Specific Rules

### Performance
- Must have prior written warnings on file
- Employee must have been given opportunity to respond
- Use procedural fairness steps (identify → notify → respond → decide → notify)
- Do NOT dismiss without documented warning trail - risk of unfair dismissal claim

### Serious Misconduct (summary dismissal - no notice)
- Must meet the Fair Work Regulations definition: wilful/deliberate behaviour, or serious risk to health/safety/reputation
- Examples: theft, fraud, assault, intoxication, refusing lawful direction
- Document the specific conduct in the letter
- Still pay out accrued leave

### Redundancy
- Job must genuinely no longer exist
- Proper consultation required (Modern Awards or NES)
- Redundancy pay applies (NES table) UNLESS small business (under 15 employees)
- Redeployment considered?

### End of Probation
- NES notice period still applies - probation does NOT override notice
- Procedural fairness still recommended (general protections risk even during probation)
- Simple language: "we have decided not to continue your employment beyond the probationary period"
