# google-ads-campaign-launch

Build and launch a Google Search campaign end to end in the current UI.

**When in the sequence:** 2nd for Google, after conversions  
**Rough time:** 2-3 hours

## Before you start, you need

- Working conversion tracking (see above)
- A landing page that matches search intent
- A keyword budget you are willing to lose while learning

## It will ask you for

- What people would actually type to find you
- Your monthly budget and target geography
- Whether the same landing page serves organic traffic too

## What it will not do

- Validate that anybody is searching for what you sell. Phase 0 does that, and it is the phase everyone skips.

## Worth knowing

Covers the settings that silently waste budget: search partners, presence-or-interest targeting, AI Max, dynamic ad groups.
